Q1. The new Terrace View Apartment building will be ten equally sized floors when completed. The first three floors have been built and the fourth floor is currently under construction. The FS survey rod was located at the face of the building. What will be the final elevation at the top of the finished tenth floor at the end of the construction project?
level
  • A) 201.15 ft
  • B) 153.85 ft
  • C) 237.85 ft
  • D) 129.85 ft
Solution:
Elevation of the Base (Ground level at the building) \(Elev_{Base} = BM1 + BS - FS\) \(Elev_{Base} = 122.50\ ft + 4.10\ ft - 8.75\ ft\) \(Elev_{Base} = 117.85\ ft\) Considering that all the floors are equally sized, we can find the height of the 3rd floor, then divide by 3 to find the height of each floor, then multiple by 10 for the height of the tenth floor. Height of third floor \(Height_{3rdFloor} = FS + H\tan(24°)\) \(Height_{3rdFloor} = 8.75\ ft + 61.20\ ft \times \tan(24°)\) \(Height_{3rdFloor} = 36.00\ ft\) Height of tenth floor \(Height_{EachFloor} = \dfrac{36}{3} = 12\ feet\) \(Height_{10thFloor} = 12\ feet \times 10\ floors = 120\ feet\) Elevation of tenth floor \(Elev_{10thFloor} = Elev_{Base} + Height_{10thFloor}\) \(Elev_{10thFloor} = 117.85\ ft + 120\ ft = 237.85\ ft\)
