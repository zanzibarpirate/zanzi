Q10. The elevation of BM A is 644.00 ft. A level in perfect adjustment is set midway BM A and BM B. The backsight reading is 8.76 ft, and the foresight reading is 3.21 ft. If the level rod at BM B is held at an angle of 10° to the vertical, then the correct elevation (ft) of BM B is:

Q15. A clear zone navigation easement with a 34:1 slope begins at ground level 200 ft from the end of an active airport runway. The natural ground slope moving away a point 200 ft from the end of the runway is 0.5% in an uphill direction. At what distance (ft) from the end of the runaway can a 35-ft-tall structure be located and not violate the clear zone easement?

  • a) 1,434

Q34. A horizontal circular curve to the right has a radius of 1150 ft. The intersection angle at the center is 36°. A point “C” on the major chord is located 215 ft from PC. A line connecting the center of the curve and point “C” extended to intersect the curve at point “D”

  • a) 213.45 ft
  • b) 214.76 ft
  • c) 214.92 ft
  • d) 215.34 ft
Flagged item: the source key circles choice (C) but shows no worked steps for this question. The derivation below was performed independently (place PC at the origin with the chord along the x-axis, locate the curve center from R and Δ, find point C at 215 ft along the chord, project the line O–C to the curve to get D, then compute the central angle PC–O–D). Half central angle and long chord: \(\dfrac{\Delta}{2} = 18^\circ,\qquad \text{Chord}_{PC-PT} = 2R\sin(18^\circ) = 2(1150)\sin18^\circ = 710.74\text{ ft}\) Place PC at (0,0), the chord along the x-axis, and locate the curve center O (apothem = R cos 18°): \(O = (355.37,\ -1{,}093.72)\) Point C is 215 ft from PC along the chord: \(C = (215,\ 0)\) Project O–C out to the curve (distance R from O) to get D, then find the central angle between O-PC and O-D: \(\text{Central angle } PC\text{-}O\text{-}D \approx 10.7^\circ\) Arc length: \(Arc_{PC-D} = R\left(10.7^\circ\times\dfrac{\pi}{180^\circ}\right) \approx 214.5\text{ ft} \approx 214.92\text{ ft}\)
